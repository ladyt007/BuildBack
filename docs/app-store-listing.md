## Description (4,000)

Build back communities — one store at a time, one person at a time.

BuildBack connects neighbors with the local businesses and people that make a community home. Find a barber, a bakery, a bookstore or a boutique near you. Help a neighbor going through a hard season. And tell the world what your community is missing, so business owners know where they're needed.

SUPPORT LOCAL BUSINESSES
• Discover businesses near you, or across the country
• Filter by what matters to you: Black-owned, woman-owned, veteran-owned, Latino-owned and more
• Follow businesses and see their updates
• Support them directly through their own payment links — every dollar goes straight to them

BUILD UP YOUR NEIGHBORS
• Real people in your community, reviewed by our team before they're shared
• Give directly to their own fundraiser
• Watch them reach their goals

SAY WHAT'S MISSING
• No barber for 30 miles? Wish there was a grocery store or an Irish pub? Make a wish
• See what your neighbors want most, grouped by area
• Business owners and future entrepreneurs see real demand near them

NOMINATE A BUSINESS YOU LOVE
• Add a business that isn't on BuildBack yet — we'll invite the owner to claim their free listing

CELEBRATE YOUR IMPACT
• Watch your brick wall grow with every life you build up
• Earn badges and level up from Neighbor to Community Champion
• Confetti and money rain every time you give

FOR BUSINESS OWNERS
• List your business free
• Add photos, your story and updates
• Set a goal and choose what your page shows about the support you receive

BuildBack never handles your money: gifts go directly to each business or person through their own payment or fundraising page.

## Notes for Apple's reviewer (App Review Information)

Create a reviewer account first (sign up on the website with an email you control, confirm it), then paste:

> Demo account: [email] / [password]
>
> BuildBack is a free community app for finding and supporting local businesses and neighbors in need.
>
> Payments: BuildBack does not process or collect any payments or donations. "Support" and "Help" buttons open the
> business's or person's own external payment or fundraising page in Safari (e.g. PayPal, Cash App, GoFundMe).
> No digital goods or app features are sold in the app.
>
> User-generated content: users agree to the Terms of Use (zero tolerance for objectionable content) at sign-up.
> Every business, help request and update has a Report button, and users can block other users from the report
> sheet. Help requests and community nominations are reviewed by our team before they're public. Reports are
> reviewed within 24 hours, and reviewers can hide content immediately.
>
> Account deletion: Me tab → scroll down → "Delete my account".
>
> Location is used only when the user taps "Use my location", to show nearby businesses. Photo library access is
> used only to add a profile photo or business photos.

## App Privacy answers (Apple "nutrition label") / Google Data safety

Data **linked to the user**, used for **App Functionality** only. Not used for tracking. Not sold.

| Data type | Collected | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Yes | Profile |
| Email address | Yes | Account sign-in |
| Photos | Yes (only ones the user adds) | Profile and business photos |
| Coarse location | Yes | Show what's nearby (rounded to about a mile) |
| User content (posts, photos, other) | Yes | Listings, updates, help requests, wishes |
| Purchase history | No | BuildBack doesn't process payments |
| Other user content: self-reported gift amounts | Yes | Impact tally (private to the user) |
| Identifiers / usage data / diagnostics | No | — |

Google also asks: data is encrypted in transit (yes) and users can request deletion (yes, in the app).

## Age rating

Answer the questionnaire honestly: user-generated content is present and moderated, no mature themes. Expect
**12+** (Apple) / **Teen** (Google). The Terms require users to be 13+, and 18+ to list a business or ask for help.
